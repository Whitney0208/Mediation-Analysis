# Mediation Analysis Results Overview

This page summarizes the current two-layer mediation analysis using the matched ADNI1 data. The analysis links a genetic exposure to corpus callosum shape and then links shape to time to AD conversion:

```text
SNP exposure -> corpus callosum shape -> AD conversion time
```

The dataset overview is available in [`data/README.md`](../data/README.md).

## Analysis sample and model

The matched sample contains 333 subjects, including 145 observed AD conversion events and 188 censored observations. The analysis uses the baseline ShapeMA corpus callosum SRVF mediator, four SNP exposures coded as additive dosage (0/1/2), and the SPLS-Cox survival variables `time` and `event`.

The main adjustment matrix is `W_pc2`. It contains an intercept, sex, standardized age, handedness, APOE4, genetic ancestry PCs 1 and 2, and education years (`PTEDUCAT`). The alternative `W_pc5` design adds ancestry PCs 3 through 5. The results summarized here use `W_pc2`.

The first layer estimates SNP-specific shape coefficient functions. The second layer uses a Cox model with joint weighted FPCA of the two shape coordinates. The main analysis retains `K = 18` shape FPCs, explaining 85.96% of the observed shape variation. The indirect effect is the functional product of the SNP-to-shape and shape-to-hazard coefficient functions. The reported total HR is the product of the direct and indirect HRs under this product-method decomposition.

## Point estimates at K = 18

The table reports HRs per one additional coded allele. Bootstrap intervals are percentile 95% CIs based on 1,000 subject-level resamples. The first two SNPs had 999 warning-free replicates; the other two had 1,000. No fatal bootstrap errors occurred.

| `rs929708` | 0.889 (0.618-1.234) | 1.070 (0.933-1.266) | 0.951 (0.666-1.354) | 0.668 |
| `rs11719939` | 1.057 (0.746-1.464) | 1.065 (0.934-1.277) | 1.127 (0.804-1.633) | 0.665 |
| `rs4639533` | 0.854 (0.608-1.205) | 1.130 (0.975-1.355) | 0.965 (0.684-1.363) | 0.665 |
| `rs2515029` | 0.972 (0.740-1.317) | 0.953 (0.816-1.075) | 0.926 (0.691-1.250) | 0.666 |

## Interpretation of the point estimates

`rs929708` has a direct HR below 1 and an indirect HR above 1. The two pathways point in opposite directions, so the total HR is close to 1. `rs4639533` shows the same pattern, with the largest positive point estimate for the shape-mediated pathway and a total HR close to 1.

`rs11719939` has direct and indirect HRs above 1, giving the largest total point estimate among the four SNPs. `rs2515029` has HRs below 1 for both pathways, with a negative indirect contribution mainly arising from the second shape coordinate.

These are directional descriptions of point estimates. Every 95% bootstrap CI for the direct, indirect, and total HR includes 1. The current analysis therefore does not provide evidence for a statistically distinguishable direct, shape-mediated, or total association at the 95% CI level.

## Bootstrap behavior

The bootstrap distributions were generated by resampling subjects, refitting the shape layer, recomputing the joint FPCA basis, and refitting the Cox layer in each replicate. The bootstrap means were close to the corresponding point estimates. For the main indirect effect, the bootstrap 95% CIs were 0.933-1.266 for `rs929708`, 0.934-1.277 for `rs11719939`, 0.975-1.355 for `rs4639533`, and 0.816-1.075 for `rs2515029`.

## Fixed-K sensitivity

The analysis was repeated with `K = 8`, 10, 12, 15, and 18. The direction of the indirect point estimate was stable across all K values: positive for `rs929708`, `rs11719939`, and `rs4639533`, and negative for `rs2515029`. The corresponding indirect point HR ranges were 1.069-1.083, 1.067-1.076, 1.111-1.129, and 0.944-0.954, respectively.

All fixed-K bootstrap 95% CIs for the indirect HR included 1. Increasing K raised the retained shape variance from 66.60% at K = 8 to 85.96% at K = 18, and the C-index increased from approximately 0.643-0.647 to approximately 0.665-0.668. The qualitative direction of the mediation estimates was stable, while uncertainty remained substantial.

## Current conclusion

The fitted models show directionally different SNP-specific mediation patterns. `rs929708` and `rs4639533` have opposing direct and shape-mediated pathways, `rs11719939` has concordant positive pathways, and `rs2515029` has concordant negative pathways. The directions are reasonably stable across the examined FPCA dimensions, but the bootstrap intervals remain wide and include the null value of HR = 1 for every reported effect.

These results are model-based associations. They do not establish a causal mediation pathway. Before final inference, the baseline diagnosis and survival origin for `RID 739`, SNP coded-allele orientation, and the prespecified handling of center and intracranial-volume effects should be documented.
